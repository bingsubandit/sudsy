# C and C++

Two languages, `c` and `cpp`, one crate (`lang-cpp`) with one hand-written lexer, one structural layer and a deep layer of lookahead. There is no parser for the full grammar: C and C++ cannot be parsed without the preprocessor and the types the headers declare, and the tree-sitter grammars and `cpp_parser` fail on many real files (unknown macros). The tree is a token-level structure in the manner of clang-format's unwrapped lines, and it never fails.

Files: `.c` and `.h` are `c`; `.cc .cpp .cxx .hpp .hh .hxx .ipp` are `cpp`. A `.h` is C because the extension does not say, and the lexer differs only in keywords (C++ keywords are identifiers in C); C++ projects that use `.h` select `cpp` with a `files` override.

## Lexer (`lexer.rs`)
Lossless tokens: identifiers, keywords (by dialect), numbers (digit separators, hex floats, suffixes), strings and chars with encoding prefixes, raw strings and literal suffixes (`"a"_sv`; in a `.c` file a name after a string is a separate token, in a `.h` it may be C++), header names after `#include`/`#include_next`/`#import`, maximal-munch punctuators, and `Other` for a stray byte. Comments and backslash-newline continuations are trivia. A `#` that starts a line begins a directive; the directive's tokens carry its id. The only errors are an unterminated block comment or raw string.

## Structure (`structure.rs`)
One pass over the tokens builds nodes over them:
- `Line`: an unwrapped line (a statement, a declaration, a `case`/label, a `public:`, a macro invocation alone on its line). A control statement is one line with its body (`if (a) b; else c;`, `do { } while (x);`); a line goes on after a `}` that is followed by `;`, `,`, `)`, `.`, `else`, `catch`, `while` of a `do`, an operator, or the `(` of a called lambda.
- `Block` (a `{ }` that holds statements or declarations), `InitList` (a `{ }` of values), `ParenGroup`, `BracketGroup`: bracket groups, nested in the line that opens them. `BraceGroup` is a `{` the text never closes (a branch of `#if`).
- `Directive`: a preprocessor directive including its continuation lines; directives are siblings of lines, never inside one.
- `PpGroup` of `PpBranch`: an `#if`...`#endif` whose branches start and end at the same bracket depth. When a branch opens or closes a bracket the others do not, only the `Directive` nodes remain.

## Deep parse (`deep/`)
A second pass reads each `Line` and bracket group by lookahead on the tokens (no rollback) and replaces it with the statement, declaration or expression it is, or leaves it opaque. The ambiguities are settled as clang-format settles them: `<` opens template arguments when a `>` follows over tokens an argument can have; a name followed by a name, or by `*`/`&` and a name, starts a declaration; a `( )` after a name is a parameter list in a declaration scope and a constructor call in a function body (`T x(a)` is a variable inside a function).
- Statements: `ExpressionStatement`, `ReturnStatement`, `IfStatement` (`Condition`), `WhileStatement`, `DoStatement`, `ForStatement`, `SwitchStatement`, `CaseClause`, `CatchClause`, `BreakStatement`, `ContinueStatement`, `GotoStatement`, `LabelStatement` (`#goto-target`: code from a label on is reachable), `MacroStatement` (`FOO(x);` or `FOO` with a capitalized name; `#inert`: a macro may expand to anything), `EmptyStatement`, `TryStatement` (`#try`: `body`, `catches`; no `finally`).
- Declarations: `SimpleDeclaration`, `FunctionDeclaration` (`ParameterList`, `Parameter`, `ConstructorInitializers` of `Initializer`s, body `Block`), `Declarator` (one declarator of a `SimpleDeclaration` or class, with the comma after it), `ClassSpecifier` (`BaseClause`, `AccessSpecifier`), `EnumSpecifier`, `NamespaceDeclaration`, `TemplateDeclaration` (`TemplateParameters`, the `declaration` field).
- Expressions: `Binary` (by the precedence lines of the grammar; the shared `Pratt` climb), `Assignment` (not `.field = v` of an initializer, not an init-capture `[x = x]`), `Ternary`, `Unary`, `Cast` (opaque), `Call` (`Arguments`), `MemberAccess`, `Index`, `QualifiedName` (`TemplateArguments`), `Parenthesized`, `Lambda` (`Captures`), `New` (`#call`), `StringConcat`, `CompoundExpression` (a comma or `case` range where one expression is expected).
- Left as `Line` (opaque): a line a directive crosses, a statement macro with a block (its block still joins by `short_functions`), GNU `({ })`. Not read: requires-clauses (skipped), `a * b;` is a declaration, the insides of parameters and conditions are partly loose parts.
- `Directive` is `#directive`: the flow graph skips it among statements, and a `switch` with a directive in its body may have arms the tree does not show (`#if`/`#include` around `case`).
- Roles, tags and fields are in `grammars/cpp.grammar`; `#part` marks what a body may hold loosely (`#part*`), so a shape check passes on tokens the parse does not place.

`#if` branches are not each parsed as clang-format does: at `#else`/`#elif` the groups opened in the branch are closed back to the depth at `#if`, and at `#endif` the state of the last branch continues (single-pass bracket reset). Some corpus files have branch-asymmetric groups.

## Formatter
`format.rules` over the shared rules formatter (`format.rs` holds only the facts: the flow keeps what no row decides, a block's `{` ends a hang, comment gaps stay). The sections:
- `[space]`: the blank between two tokens (separators, brackets, `::`/`.`/`->`, keyword parentheses, template brackets, unary, binary, assignment and conditional operators, `operator` names); the default is `keep`, so a pair no row names stays as written. `*`, `&` and `&&` are `keep` except where the tree shows a declarator: then `pointer_alignment` (`right`, `left`, `middle`; `derive_pointer_alignment` takes the side most of the file's declarators use, as clang-format's `DerivePointerAlignment`, and `pointer_alignment` where both sides tie) decides the blank before the first mark and the blank behind the last one when a name follows. A mark is a declarator when it is in a parameter, a function or a variable declaration, a line the parse left as `Line`, a directive or a function-pointer group, in the type of a cast or in template arguments, follows a type (a name, a keyword, `>` or another mark) and is not `operator*`; in template arguments a `>`, `,`, `)` or `...` must follow (`Foo<N * 2>` is a product). Everything else stays as written: a class head (`class A : B<T *>`), a function type (`void (*f)(int *)`), the arguments of `sizeof(void *)`, a declarator after a comma, a `*` or `&` in an expression the parse may have taken for a declaration. A `*`, `&` or `&&` of a Binary node gets a blank on both sides (`operator_gap`) unless it follows a type, a mark or a closing parenthesis and a name, a mark or the end of a type follows it, in a parent that does not prove an expression (`binary_clear`: assignment, return, nested binary, conditional, index, unary): `a*b+1` and `x = a*b` change, `T * x`, `(T)&x` and `sizeof(T **)` stay. `braced_list_padding` sets the blank inside the braces of an initializer list (`{1, 2}` or `{ 1, 2 }`; the Mozilla, WebKit and GNU presets pad); an empty list is `{}`, and a list that holds a `;` (a lambda body the parse took for a list) stays as written. The blank after the `)` of a cast is `space_after_cast` (a `*`, `&`, `&&`, `+` or `-` behind it stays as written: the parse may have taken `(a) * b` for a cast); the marks of a range `for` and of a `catch` are declarator marks (`pointer_in_loose`), a mark behind a reference is none (`it && *it`); a word of `pointer_qualifier_words` behind a mark is no name (`const & noexcept`). A conversion operator with a ref-qualifier (`operator T &() & noexcept { }`) is a function; `+` and `-` between a name or number and a name or number inside `[ ]` have blanks.
- `[joined]`: two tokens that stay on one line (the `}` of an initializer list stays with its last value unless a comment, a directive or a `;` is between).
- `[flow]`, `[at_least_one]`: a `}` of an initializer list on its own line closes at the indentation of its line; a `,` of an initializer list and the `?` and `:` of a conditional keep source alignment but have at least one blank.
- `[layout]`: the document (blank lines up to `max_blank_lines`), a directive at column 0 after its comments (comments right above it that start at column 0 stay there), `#if` groups one item per line (a branch of `#if 0` or `#elif 0` is kept as written, only its directive line is re-spaced), a namespace body flat (`namespace_indentation`), `public:` outdented (`access_modifier_outdent`), a `case` outdented unless `indent_case_labels`, a goto label at column 0 unless `indent_goto_labels`, `if`, loops and their `else` as `compound` (`short_ifs`, `short_loops`), a `{` on its own line by `brace_wrapping` (the constructs of each word are the `attach` kind sets of that option in lang.yaml; a block right after a call, `name(args) {`, is a `function` unless the name is in `for_each_macros` (`control`); `split_empty_braces` keeps `{` `}` of an empty body on two lines; with no word set an unknown construct's `{` attaches), `// clang-format off` and `on` bracket a region kept as written (the shared ignore mechanism), with the comments after the marker and the indentation of its first line,, blanks after `#` stripped unless `indent_pp_directives: keep`, leading blank lines of the file kept, and function bodies joined to the head when they fit (`short_functions`; kept expanded under a template header that has its line, and after a multi-line signature that has a comment; a header that is too wide for the line moves the body below it; a directive in the signature keeps it expanded, and so does a body that holds a block of its own; a `name(args) {` macro block joins like a function body unless the name is a `for_each_macros` loop), lambda and enum bodies as written when short (`short_lambdas`, `short_enums`).
Trailing comments follow `spaces_before_trailing_comments` and, with `align_trailing_comments`, line up over consecutive lines (a run ends at a line without a comment, at a change between a preprocessor line and code, and where a comment would pass `line_width`); a block comment written against code stays against it, `/*name=*/` against the next token. The body of a `#define` and the condition of an `#if` or `#elif` are read with the grammar of the file, in the tree for formatting only (`SourceFile::for_format`; lint and the plain parse do not pay for it), and laid out as code (`directive-payload-layout`); a body of one token stays as it is: a body of one line stays on the line of its name when it fits `line_width`, otherwise it breaks after the name; a body of several statements or with a block goes on indented lines, the `\` aligned by `align_escaped_newlines` (a tab counts to the next stop of eight); a comment behind the body takes no room; a body whose brackets do not pair stays text. Source line breaks inside a statement of a body stay (clang-format joins them). `template <...>` follows `break_template_declarations`. A braced list with five or more commas is laid out in columns as clang-format does (`column_list_min_commas`: cells padded to the widest of their column, the number of columns the fewest that gives the fewest rows within the width; with a trailing comma or a comment the list breaks after `{`, otherwise its first row stays on the `{` line and the others stand under it; a designated initializer, a blank line, a comment in front of an item or a brace in an item keeps the list as written; with `indent_style: tab` and `align_with_tabs` the padding is tabs up to the last tab stop, a single blank stays a blank). A declaration head that has a line comment goes on with the continuation indent (`comment_continue`), and where commented parameters do not fit behind a return type of several words the name goes on a line of its own (`name_break`, `name_break_after`). A declaration head that a directive splits behind a parenthesized group (`__declspec(dllexport)` `#endif` `int f(...)`) stays as written up to its `{` (`keep_split_declarations`). A function in a macro body (`type name(params) {`) is a declaration, a `##` paste is one name, and the marks of a parenthesized group are declarator marks where an item of the group is a declaration (`pointer_shaped`). Source line breaks, blank lines and wrapped lines stay where they are; a wrapped line is indented by the difference of the source indentation to the line its statement starts on (`source_hang`), counted from the line the hung flow starts on, so a wrapped call keeps its alignment under a chain. `align_with_tabs` lets `indent_style: tab` align with tabs and spaces. Not yet laid out by the formatter: joining or breaking lines that the source writes differently (wrapping to `line_width`, constructor initializer lists, calls and binary expressions), and column alignment of declarations (the plan: [../design/cpp-formatter.md](../design/cpp-formatter.md)).

## Parity
`scripts/parity.sh format clang-format` runs clang-format 20.1.8 (`tests/parity/clang-format/`, a `.clang-format` per style: each corpus project's own for LLVM, fmt, range-v3 and Linux, LLVM for folly and SQLite, the six presets on the bench inputs; sorting of includes, namespace end comments and comment reflow off) and compares it with `sudsy format` on the corpus of `fetch-cpp-corpus.sh`. Files equal under the comparison without line wrapping (the ratchets of `differences.yaml`, which may only rise): LLVM 345/420, folly 1,936/2,299, Linux 2,318/2,872, range-v3 394/593, fmt 46/70, SQLite 110/325, google 2/10, chromium 2/10, mozilla 1/10, webkit 1/10, 0/10 for the other presets. Second format and refused: 0 in every project.
