## Priority

Weight: the primary tool of a leg 3, a popular secondary 2, any other 1. Points: to the 85% bar. Needs: the findings (lint) or files (format, real-world corpus groups only) still to win; causes: the largest until the need is met (a rest no cause names is `unclassified`). Difficulty: per cause 1 when its rule exists and is mapped in the catalog, 3 for a new rule, a format gap or an unclassified rest. Score: weight x points / difficulty. A leg at the bar is not listed.
